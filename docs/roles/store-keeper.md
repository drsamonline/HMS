# Store keeper

You buy what the hospital needs and you know where it is. The software is the answer to
"did we order it, did it arrive, and who has it now".

## What you see when you log in

**Stores.** Stock and purchasing.

## Your first hour

1. Open **Inventory** and find one item you know is in the store.
2. Open **Procurement** and see what is on order.
3. Receive a test delivery against an order.

## Your first week

- Learn the difference between receiving a delivery and adjusting a count. One is a
  fact about the world; the other is your opinion about the records.
- Learn to check an order against what arrived before you receive it. Receiving what
  did not arrive is how a store loses money quietly.
- Learn which items are consumable and which are equipment. They leave the store
  differently.

## What you must never do

- **Never adjust a stock figure to make it match the shelf without saying why.** The
  discrepancy is the information; erasing it erases the reason it happened.
- **Never receive goods against the wrong purchase order** to clear a backlog.
- **Never approve your own purchase order** where the hospital's rules require a
  second signature.

## What the software will not let you do

You cannot dispense to a patient, prescribe or bill. You cannot delete a stock
movement.

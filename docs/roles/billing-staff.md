# Billing staff

Money is the part of a hospital where a small error becomes a large argument. The
software's job is to make every charge traceable to something that happened.

## What you see when you log in

**Billing.** Invoices and receipts.

## Your first hour

1. Open **Patients** and find a patient with a completed visit.
2. Raise a test invoice with one line item.
3. Record a part payment against it, then open the invoice and see the balance.

## Your first week

- Learn that invoice and receipt numbers come from sequences the hospital configured.
  They do not repeat. If one does, stop - it is a continuity problem, not a display
  problem.
- Learn that a receipt is not an invoice and a credit note is neither.
- Learn to print a bill. The patient leaves with it, and the print is the version that
  matters in a dispute.

## What you must never do

- **Never edit a finalised invoice to correct an amount.** Raise a credit note. An
  invoice that changes after the patient has a copy is indefensible.
- **Never record a payment that has not been received.** A receipt is a statement that
  money exists.
- **Never back-date a receipt** to make a month look better. The trail records when it
  was entered as well as the date you typed.

## What the software will not let you do

You cannot open clinical records, prescribe, or dispense. You cannot delete an
invoice. You cannot change a receipt number.

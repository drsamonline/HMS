# Operational Year Basis

HHMS supports two operational-year modes:

- **Calendar year:** 1 January through 31 December.
- **Financial year:** configurable start month/day; default 1 April.

The actual clinical timestamp remains authoritative and is never rewritten when the basis changes.

The operational year drives case renewal, annual archive boundaries, historical-year labeling, and related annual workflows.

A change of basis affects future calculations. Existing clinical records retain their original timestamps and stored operational-year values.

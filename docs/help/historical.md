# Historical Data

Records from closed operational years live here: live, online archive and cold
archive, with the rules in `docs/ARCHIVE_RETENTION_TIERS.md`.

Archived is not deleted. A record past its retention date is archivable, never
removable - three years is a floor, not a ceiling.

Finding a record here takes longer than in the live data. That is the trade the
archive makes, and it is deliberate.

# How {{HOSPITAL NAME}} uses your personal and health information

Version 1.0 · Effective {{DATE}}

## Who we are

{{HOSPITAL NAME}}, {{ADDRESS}}. We are the hospital that holds your medical record. For anything in this notice, write to {{GRIEVANCE OFFICER NAME}}, {{DESIGNATION}}, at {{EMAIL}} or {{PHONE}}.

## What we collect, and why

We collect only what we need to treat you and to run the hospital.

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your name, age or date of birth, gender, blood group, mobile number and address | To identify you and to contact you about your care |
| Your symptoms, case history, examination findings and diagnosis | To treat you |
| Prescriptions, remedies, potencies and follow-up notes | To continue your treatment safely |
| Investigation orders and results | To diagnose and to monitor |
| Admission, ward and discharge details, if you are admitted | To care for you as an inpatient |
| Bills, payments and outstanding amounts | To bill you and to keep our accounts |
| Documents you give us, such as earlier reports | To keep one complete record |

## Who sees it

Your record is seen by the doctors, nurses and staff who are treating you or administering your care, and by the billing and records staff who need it for their work. Access is by named login and is recorded.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

We share it outside the hospital only when: you ask us to; another doctor or hospital needs it for your treatment; the law requires it, for example a statutory notification or a court order; or a service provider processes it for us under a contract that binds them to our instructions.

## Where it is kept

On our own server, inside this hospital. It is not stored in the cloud, and our software does not send it anywhere.

## How long we keep it

{{STATE THE PERIOD, e.g. "Your medical record is kept for X years from your last visit, as required of hospitals."}} Bills and payment records are kept for the period required by tax law. When the period ends, the record is {{deleted / anonymised}}, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

## Your rights

You may ask us to:

- **show you** what we hold about you, and give you a summary;
- **correct** anything that is wrong or out of date;
- **erase** your data, where no law requires us to keep it — for a medical record this is limited by our duty to keep records;
- **nominate** someone to exercise these rights for you if you cannot;
- **complain** to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these, write to {{GRIEVANCE OFFICER}} at {{EMAIL}} or {{PHONE}}. We acknowledge within {{24 hours}} and resolve within {{15 days}}.

## If you are a parent or guardian

We record the treatment of children. Where we process a child's data we ask for your consent as a parent or guardian, except where the law lets us treat the child without it — for example in a medical emergency.

## Consent

We rely on your consent for the processing described here, and on the legal grounds available to a hospital for treatment and for records we are required to keep. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to {{GRIEVANCE OFFICER}}; withdrawing it may limit what we can do for you, and does not undo processing already carried out.

## Changes

If we change this notice, we publish the new version here with its effective date, and tell you at your next visit.
